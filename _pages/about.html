Welcome to my webpage

Hi, I'm Paul.

I am currently a doctoral researcher/PhD candidate in Mathematics, at both the University of Nantes (NU) and the Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB), under the supervision of Guillaume Poly (NU) and Yvik Swan (ULB).

My research interests lie in probability theory, with a particular focus on Gaussian analysis, limit theorems, and quantitative methods for distributional approximation, such as Stein's method. More broadly, I am interested in exploring how probabilistic and analytic techniques can help characterize the asymptotic behavior and geometric features of random objects.
