Welcome to my webpage

Hi, I'm Paul.

I am currently a doctoral researcher/PhD candidate in Mathematics, at both the University of Nantes (NU) and the Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB), under the supervision of Guillaume Poly (NU) and Yvik Swan (ULB).

My research focuses on limit theorems in probability arising in a variety of settings, with particular emphasis on Stein’s method, among other approaches.
