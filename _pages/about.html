Welcome to my webpage

Hi, I'm Paul.

I am currently a doctoral researcher/PhD candidate in Mathematics, at both the University of Nantes (NU) and the Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB), under the supervision of Guillaume Poly (NU) and Yvik Swan (ULB).

My research interests lie in probability theory, with a particular focus on Gaussian analysis, limit theorems, and quantitative methods for distributional approximation, such as Stein's method.

During my doctorate, I used probabilistic and analytical techniques to investigate the asymptotic behavior and geometric properties of random objects. My research spanned a broad range of topics, including maxima of i.i.d. random variables, non-linear functionals of Gaussian fields, information-theoretic functionals, random polynomials, and nodal sets of stochastic processes. In particular, I developed and applied tools from Malliavin calculus, $\Gamma$-calculus, and Stein’s method to study their asymptotic properties in the form of probabilistic quantitative limit theorems. I also used the Kac–Rice formula and related techniques to investigate the geometry of nodal sets, including the number and distribution of zeros.

More broadly, I am interested in exploring how probabilistic and analytic techniques can help characterize the asymptotic behavior and geometric features of random objects.
